⚡ Peak Energy plans first US sodium-ion gigafactory in Sacramento

Peak Energy will invest up to $71m in a Sacramento plant producing up to 4 GWh a year of sodium-ion storage systems — billed as the first of its kind in the US. Domestic sodium manufacturing would cut reliance on lithium and imported cells for grid storage.

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ UK's Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant rises to £9,000 for oil and LPG homes

From 21 July the heat-pump grant jumped 20% from £7,500 to £9,000 for the roughly 1.7 million UK homes on oil or LPG. The boost targets rural households hardest hit by fossil-fuel price swings and aims to reignite lagging adoption.

Source: Installer Online

Adoption growth slows below the pace needed for 2030 targets

UK heat-pump installs hit a record 125,037 in 2025 but growth eased to 27% — under the ~33% compound rate required to reach 450,000 a year by 2030. The Clean Heat Market Mechanism now requires boiler makers to sell heat pumps worth 8% of boiler sales or face penalties.

Source: HeatPumpsAndSolar

💧 Hydro

⚡ Spain awards €165m for 2 GW of pumped-hydro storage

Spain allocated €165m to seven pumped-hydro projects totalling ~2.1 GW / 21 GWh across six regions, six of them new reversible plants. It is a substantial push toward Spain's goal of 5 GW of pumped storage by 2040.

Source: pv magazine

Global pumped storage passes 200 GW — the 'year of the water battery'

Global installed hydropower reached 1,469 GW in 2025 after 28 GW of additions, including a record 11.6 GW of pumped storage, taking cumulative pumped capacity past 200 GW for the first time. Long-duration storage is scaling just as variable renewables need it.

Source: International Hydropower Association
