☀️ Solar

⚡ JinkoSolar claims 35.24% world record for perovskite/TOPCon tandem cell

JinkoSolar says it has pushed a perovskite-on-TOPCon tandem cell to 35.24% efficiency, a new record for this architecture. Because TOPCon is now the dominant mass-production cell technology, a tandem built on top of it is one of the most direct routes to getting 30%+ modules onto factory lines.

⚡ MicroQuanta and Zhejiang University certify 23.9% perovskite mini-module

A MicroQuanta-led team certified 23.9% steady-state efficiency on a 19.48 cm² perovskite module, with an 83.3% fill factor — which they say is the highest yet for a perovskite module. Aging tests also showed their cooling step cut fill-factor losses to under a third, chipping away at perovskite's durability problem.

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ India approves ₹14,500 crore scheme for 92 GWh of energy storage

India's cabinet approved viability-gap funding for 92 GWh of storage through FY2030-31: 60 GWh pumped hydro, 30 GWh batteries and 2 GWh emerging tech, unlocking roughly ₹97,600 crore of investment. Battery projects must meet a 40% domestic-content rule, a big push for local cell and pack manufacturing.
