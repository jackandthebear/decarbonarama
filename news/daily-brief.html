⚡ Africa's largest solar-plus-storage project enters full commercial operation in Egypt

The Obelisk project in southern Egypt has gone fully commercial with 1.1 GW of solar capacity paired with 200 MWh of battery storage — the biggest combined solar-and-battery build on the continent to date. It is a useful proof point that gigawatt-scale hybrid plants are now routine engineering rather than a demonstration, and that the economics stack up in high-irradiance emerging markets.

The Federal Communications Commission has banned newly imported internationally manufactured power inverters from being used in US solar PV and energy storage projects, citing supply-chain and cybersecurity risk to the grid.
