☀️ Solar

⚡ UNSW and UtmoLight set 23.5% record for large perovskite submodule

Researchers certified a stabilised 23.5% efficiency on a 676 cm² perovskite submodule, 0.6 points above the previous benchmark, by removing the nickel oxide layer that can destabilise the material. The team now plans a 2.8 m² full-size module targeting roughly 18–19%. Reported 7 October.

Source: pv magazine

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ India's largest grid-scale vanadium flow battery awarded

Bondada Engineering won the EPC contract for a 20 MW/120 MWh vanadium redox flow system at GIPCL's Vadodara gas plant complex, with ten years of O&M and completion 18 months after award. Long-duration flow chemistry is gaining a foothold in India's storage build-out.

Source: SolarQuarter

Fridge-sized batteries cut bills for Brockton businesses

Massachusetts firm Powertown will install 125–250 kW batteries at four Brockton businesses at no upfront cost, sharing the savings. One laundry firm expects to save about $30,000 a year, showing the commercial case for behind-the-meter storage.

Source: The Boston Globe

💨 Wind

Baltic Power finishes installing all 76 Vestas V236-15 MW turbines

Global Wind Service has completed turbine installation at the roughly 1.2 GW Polish project from ORLEN and Northland Power, which is now in final commissioning after first power. It is a major step for Poland's first offshore wind farm.

Source: Ocean News

DEME wins turbine installation contract for Zeevonk

DEME will transport and install 69 turbines at the ~1 GW Dutch North Sea project owned by Vattenfall and Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners, with work in 2028–29. The project is still subject to a final investment decision.

Source: Cyprus Shipping News

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ NRC approves first US commercial small modular reactor permit

On 29 September the US NRC granted TVA a construction permit for a 300 MW GE Vernova Hitachi BWRX-300 at Clinch River, Tennessee, after a 14-month review. TVA still needs a separate operating licence, but it is a landmark for US SMRs. (Slightly older than 48 hours, included as the key policy story still driving coverage.)

Source: EnergyNow

♨️ Heat Pumps

European residential heat pump sales up 11% in H1 2026

EHPA reports sales in 12 countries rose to about 1.16 million units, helped by high fossil fuel prices after the Strait of Hormuz closure and a Commission proposal to cut electricity taxes. The association stresses growth remains heavily dependent on electricity prices. Published 28 September.

Source: EHPA

💧 Hydro

Pumped storage passes 200 GW worldwide

The IHA's World Hydropower Outlook says a record 11.7 GW of pumped storage was commissioned in 2025, with 243 GW more under construction, though financing, permitting and transmission remain bottlenecks. (Published July; no major hydro news found in the last 48 hours.)

Source: Renewable Energy World

🌊 Wave & Tidal

Orbital Marine Power's four-turbine Orkney array clears EU review

The European Commission issued a Go Decision for the EURO-TIDES project, allowing Orbital to build an array of three O2-X turbines plus the existing O2 at EMEC, powering about 9,200 homes. Deployment targets 2028, contingent on closing Orbital's investment round.

Source: CompositesWorld
