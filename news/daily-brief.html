☀️ Solar

Qcells to use U.S.-made aluminium frames on residential panels

Qcells is partnering with Aluko Group to source aluminium frames made in Tennessee for its residential panel range. The move could help the panels qualify for domestic-content bonuses, tightening the U.S. solar supply chain.

Source: pv magazine

France to ease grid connection for solar-plus-storage up to 25 MW

France plans to simplify grid connection rules for solar-plus-storage projects up to 25 MW, provided grid injection stays at or below 17 MW. Faster, simpler hybrid connections should help co-located batteries scale.

Source: pv magazine

Brazil's new curtailment rules unlikely to revive utility-scale solar

Experts expect few new utility-scale projects in Brazil because the rules add some regulatory clarity but leave curtailment and financial risks largely unresolved. It is a reminder that grid policy can matter as much as panel prices.

Source: pv magazine

Three large four-hour projects, from Enertrag and Enlight, Vattenfall and Fluence, and Giga Storage, are moving ahead. Longer-duration batteries are becoming the German grid's answer to solar and wind variability.

💨 Wind

China's wind industry braces for renewed price wars

Domestic turbine orders fell 25% in the first half of 2026, the third straight half-year decline, and JPMorgan says Chinese wind stocks have dropped by as much as half since the end of Q1. Subsidy phase-outs and grid curtailment are squeezing the world's largest wind market, with knock-on effects for global turbine pricing.

Source: Energy Connects

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ U.S. reviving three shuttered nuclear plants

Palisades (Michigan), Crane on Three Mile Island (Pennsylvania) and Duane Arnold (Iowa) are being restarted with Department of Energy loans of up to $4.4 billion, restoring about 2.25 GW. Restarts are targeted from March 2027 to early 2029, driven by data-centre demand, with Microsoft and Google contracting for power from two of the plants.

Source: WSLS / AP

Uzbekistan plans second and third nuclear plants

President Mirziyoyev said talks with potential investors on a second plant were due on 17 October. The first plant, under construction in Jizzakh with Rosatom, is planned at 2,110 MW and about $9.5 billion; no details have been given on the new ones.

Source: Gazeta.uz
