⚡ India targets 530 GW of renewables by 2030 as module manufacturing hits 225 GW

Citing an IEA report, India's renewable capacity has passed 300 GW and is projected to reach 530 GW by 2030, with domestic module capacity of about 225 GW a year making it the second-largest producer. Utility-scale solar tariffs are said to be roughly 40% below coal-based power, and grid and market reforms could lift additions by over 20%.

Source: SolarQuarter

It shows data-centre demand pulling clean power build-out.

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ Canada details new National Heat Pump Rebate

Households can claim $2,000, or up to $10,000 for low- to median-income homes, with no energy audit needed. The portal opens in early 2027, but installs from 1 November 2026 may qualify retroactively, and the programme is expected to run until March 2034.

Source: Mechanical Business
