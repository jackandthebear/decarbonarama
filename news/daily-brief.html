☀️ Solar

⚡ US on track for record 43.4 GW of utility-scale solar in 2026

The EIA forecasts a record 43.4 GW of new utility-scale solar PV this year — a 60% jump on 2025 and 51% of all new US generating capacity. Texas leads with roughly 40% of additions, including the 837 MW Tehuacana Creek 1 project, the country's largest expected online this year.

Source: PV Tech

Perovskite modules enter mass production as tandems push past 34%

GCL says 2026 is its first year of mass-producing perovskite modules from a 500 MW line installed last year, with shipments expected by the end of Q3. Meanwhile LONGi's NREL-certified 34.85% perovskite-silicon tandem record stands past the single-junction theoretical limit, and commercial modules now sit in the 24–29% efficiency range.

Source: Perovskite-Info

Origis brings another ~500 MW online at Rockhound complex in Texas

Origis Energy has started commercial operations at three further phases of its Rockhound Solar complex in Ector County, adding roughly 500 MWdc. It's a marker of how quickly the Permian Basin is becoming a solar heavyweight alongside its oil legacy.

Source: pv magazine USA

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ CATL's sodium-ion grid batteries start shipping this month

CATL's TENER sodium storage system — billed as the world's first field-validated sodium-ion BESS — begins customer deliveries in September, with around 1 GWh due to ship by year-end. A 60 GWh three-year supply deal with HyperStrong, the largest sodium-ion order to date, underpins the ramp. Sodium-ion is now a real, lithium-free option for grid storage.

Source: Battery-Tech Network

UK backs Agratas Somerset gigafactory with £380m grant

Britain has approved £380m for Tata-owned Agratas from the Advanced Propulsion Centre to help build its 40 GWh gigafactory in Somerset. It's the biggest single commitment yet to domestic UK cell manufacturing and a key plank of the country's EV supply chain.

Source: Battery-Tech Network

Colorado becomes first US state to mandate EV battery recycling

Colorado has banned disposal of EV batteries and set mineral-recovery targets, the first mandate of its kind in the US. It lands as the global EV battery recycling market hits $1.4bn — forecast to grow ~45% a year to $19bn by 2033 — and as Redwood Materials repurposes 100 used Rivian packs to store energy at the carmaker's Illinois factory.

Source: Resource Recycling

💨 Wind

US offshore wind projects report strong progress after work resumes

Vineyard Wind 1 now has 60 of 62 turbines fully installed with over 50 exporting power, Ørsted has 60 of 65 turbines up at Revolution Wind plus the first five at Sunrise Wind, and Coastal Virginia's fabrication is well advanced. A rare run of good news for a sector battered by US federal headwinds.

Source: The Maritime Executive

First 14 MW turbine stands at East Anglia THREE

ScottishPower and Masdar have installed the first Siemens Gamesa 14 MW turbine at East Anglia THREE off Suffolk — 262 m tall with a 236 m rotor. When complete, the 1.4 GW farm will be among the UK's largest, powering the equivalent of 1.3 million homes.

Source: Enlit World

RWE installs world's first turbine with CO₂-reduced steel and recyclable blades

At Denmark's Thor offshore wind farm, RWE has erected the first turbine combining a low-carbon steel tower with fully recyclable rotor blades — tackling wind's own embodied-carbon and end-of-life challenges.

Source: RWE

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ Tennessee issues licence for a fusion plant

Tennessee regulators issued a licence for a fusion power plant on 1 September — one of the first such state-level approvals anywhere, and a sign that fusion is starting to move from lab milestone to regulatory reality.

Source: World Nuclear News

Fuel loading under way for Palisades restart

Holtec announced on 1 September that fuel loading has begun at Palisades in Michigan, the first US plant to be revived after full shutdown. A successful restart would set a template for returning retired reactors to the grid.

Source: World Nuclear News

Thea Energy teams with Nvidia on stellarator digital twin; marine SMR proposed in Canada

Thea Energy is working with Nvidia and Synopsys on a full digital twin of its Helios stellarator fusion plant concept, while in Canada a marine-based SMR plant was proposed this week — two glimpses of how varied the advanced-nuclear pipeline has become.

Source: ANS Nuclear Newswire

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ £9,000 heat pump grants now live for oil and LPG homes

Under the enhanced Boiler Upgrade Scheme running to 31 March 2027, off-gas-grid homes in England and Wales heated by oil or LPG can now claim £9,000 towards an air- or ground-source heat pump — £1,500 above the standard grant and the highest support level yet. These homes are among the most expensive and carbon-intensive to heat, so the targeting matters.

Source: Aira

Boiler Upgrade Scheme passes 100,000 applications

The BUS has now logged over 100,000 applications since 2022, with monthly volumes above 3,500. 2026 rule changes — relaxed EPC requirements, installer-led applications, and newly eligible air-to-air systems and heat batteries — are widening access further.

Source: Energy Saving Trust

The IHA's World Hydropower Outlook reports global pumped storage capacity has crossed 200 GW for the first time, after a record 11.6 GW added in a single year. The development pipeline now includes 621 GW of pumped storage — with China alone building 218 GW.

Source: International Hydropower Association

US Senate approves record hydropower funding for FY2026

The Senate's FY2026 spending package carries the largest federal investment in hydropower and pumped storage programmes since 2008, as a new report argues pumped storage is essential to powering the AI data-centre build-out.

Source: International Water Power

🌊 Wave & Tidal

CorPower's C4 earns first-ever DNV certificate for a wave energy converter

CorPower Ocean's C4 device, running off Aguçadoura in Portugal since 2023, has received the first DNV Prototype Certificate awarded to a wave energy converter — a credibility milestone the sector has chased for years, opening the door to bankable commercial arrays.

Source: Offshore Industry

Tidal stream pipeline strongest in the technology's history

Analysts put the tidal market at 0.68 GW in 2026, heading for nearly 3 GW by 2031. MeyGen's four turbines have generated 84 GWh+ since 2018, and Inyanga's 20 MW HydroWing array at Morlais off Anglesey is among Europe's largest consented projects moving toward deployment.

Source: reNEWS
