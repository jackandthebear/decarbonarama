☀️ Solar

⚡ JinkoSolar claims 35.24% world record for perovskite/TOPCon tandem cell

JinkoSolar says its perovskite-on-TOPCon tandem cell has hit 35.24% efficiency, a new record for this architecture. Because TOPCon is today's mainstream mass-production cell, stacking perovskite on top of it is one of the most commercially plausible routes past silicon's practical efficiency ceiling.

Source: TaiyangNews

⚡ MicroQuanta and Zhejiang University certify 23.9% perovskite mini-module

A 19.48 cm² perovskite mini-module reached a certified steady-state efficiency of 23.9% with an 83.3% fill factor, verified by China's NPVM and published in Nature Communications. Small-cell records are common, but module-level records matter more because they show the efficiency can survive scale-up.

Source: pv magazine / Now.Solar

SMA Solar narrows 2026 guidance after strong nine months

The German inverter maker tightened full-year sales guidance to €1,675–1,725m and EBITDA to €205–230m, after nine-month EBITDA rose to €136m from €115m. Its order backlog stands at about €1.86bn, a sign that European solar and storage demand is holding up.

Source: PV Tech

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ US energy storage passes 200 GWh installed

According to the SEIA/Benchmark Q4 outlook, cumulative US storage has crossed 200 GWh, with 14.3 GWh added in Q3 and 48.3 GWh year-to-date (up 20% year on year). The 2030 forecast was raised 3.7% to 708 GWh, so batteries are now a core part of how the US grid absorbs solar.

Source: WiseVoter

X-Elio picks Canadian Solar's e-Storage for NSW battery project

Brookfield-owned X-Elio will use e-Storage SolBank 3.0 units (5 MWh each) under a 22-year service agreement for a battery in New South Wales, with commercial operation expected within two years. It's another long-duration contract in Australia's fast-growing grid-storage market.

Source: pv magazine Australia / Now.Solar

Sodium-ion closes in on lithium-ion for utility-scale storage

Recent analysis says sodium-ion cells are nearing cost parity with LFP, with roughly 70 GWh already covered by supply agreements. Sodium avoids lithium price swings and performs better in the cold, which makes it an attractive hedge for grid storage.

Source: pv magazine

💨 Wind

⚡ Philippines and Ming Yang sign deal to boost offshore wind

President Marcos signed an agreement with Chinese turbine maker Ming Yang, which plans a Philippine service hub for turbine repair and maintenance. Wind supplies only about 1% of the country's grid from just over 500 MW, against an estimated 178 GW of offshore potential.

Source: The Manila Times

Ming Yang eyes Philippine turbine service centre

The plan came up at a 20 November meeting with Marcos, alongside wider trade roundtables with Chinese manufacturers. Having local O&M capacity is often what lets an emerging offshore wind market move from plans to built projects.

Source: The Philippine Business and News

Vineyard Wind and Revolution Wind both at full power

Vineyard Wind 1 (62 turbines, 800 MW, enough for about 400,000 homes) is now the largest operating US offshore wind farm, and Revolution Wind (704 MW) reached full power the same week. Together they give US offshore wind real operating scale despite federal headwinds.

Source: WBUR

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ New York releases Advanced Nuclear Master Plan targeting 5 GW

Governor Hochul unveiled a statewide roadmap for 5 GW of new nuclear, with 30 recommendations from 12 studies by NYSERDA and DPS. Separately, NYPA shortlisted 10 of 21 respondents for its first new nuclear plant, one of the most concrete US state-led nuclear pushes so far.

Source: Neutron Bytes

⚡ Pacific Fusion wins first New Mexico tritium licence for a private fusion firm

New Mexico's environment department licensed Pacific Fusion to possess and use tritium at its $1bn Albuquerque campus, the first such licence it has issued to a private fusion company. The company's demonstration system is aiming for net facility gain by 2030.

Source: World Nuclear News

Andhra Pradesh approves exploring SMRs to replace thermal plants

A state government order backs siting small modular reactors at retiring coal units once the technology matures, with private-sector participation allowed. No capacity or cost figures were given, but reusing coal sites (with their existing grid connections) is a model many countries are studying.

Source: Business Standard

♨️ Heat Pumps

New England Heat Pump Accelerator thriving after year one

The five-state programme, backed by $450m in federal Climate Pollution Reduction Grants, now has more than 25,000 participating homes toward a goal of nearly 580,000 heat pumps in four years. Incentives run to $650 for space heating and $300 for heat pump water heaters.

Source: Canary Media

European heat pump sales up 11% in first half of 2026

EHPA data show residential sales across 12 countries reached about 1.16 million units in H1, up from 1.05 million. The association credits higher oil and gas prices and planned EU electricity tax cuts, which suggests the 2024 slump has reversed.

Source: Cooling Post
