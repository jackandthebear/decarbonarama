☀️ Solar

⚡ LONGi hits 35.5% tandem cell efficiency — a new world record

At the 2026 Solar and Energy Storage Innovation Conference, LONGi's crystalline silicon-perovskite tandem cell was certified at 35.5% by the European Solar Test Installation, up from 35.2% earlier this year. Tandem cells carry a theoretical ceiling near 43%, well beyond the ~33.7% single-junction limit — a signpost for where mainstream panels are headed.

Peak Energy will invest up to $71 million in a Sacramento facility producing up to 4 GWh of sodium-ion storage systems per year — a notable step in onshoring US battery manufacturing away from lithium.
