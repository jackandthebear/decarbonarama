☀️ Solar

⚡ Perovskite-silicon tandem cell hits record 34% efficiency

Researchers reported a perovskite-silicon tandem solar cell reaching roughly 34% conversion efficiency with record-level open-circuit voltage, pushing past the practical ceiling of single-junction silicon. Tandem designs like this are seen as the most realistic near-term path to cheaper, higher-output panels once manufacturers can stabilize the perovskite layer for outdoor lifetimes.

Source: Interesting Engineering

⚡ Smoother perovskite films lift triple-junction cell to 30.1%

A materials team improved the crystallization process for perovskite films, reducing surface defects and lifting a three-layer (triple-junction) solar cell to 30.1% efficiency. Cleaner films are one of the main levers for closing the gap between lab-record and commercially deployable perovskite modules.

Source: Tech Xplore

US rooftop solar sales keep sliding

Residential solar installers are reporting a rough stretch as the loss of federal tax credits and higher financing costs squeeze new sign-ups, with analysts projecting installs could fall around a third year-over-year. It's a reminder that hardware progress and market health are two different stories right now.

Source: Inside Climate News

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ Australia's Orana battery reaches full output — third-biggest on the grid

Akaysha Energy's 415 MW / 1,660 MWh Orana battery in New South Wales, built from 448 Tesla Megapacks, has reached full commercial output, becoming the third-largest battery on Australia's National Electricity Market. The AU$650 million project was financed by an 11-bank syndicate and backed by a 12-year offtake deal with EnergyAustralia.

Source: Energy-Storage.News

Tesla and NatPower sign 25 GWh Megapack deal for Italy and the UK

Tesla will supply more than 25 GWh of Megapack battery systems to NatPower for grid-storage projects across Italy and the UK, one of the largest European battery-storage supply agreements to date. It underscores how fast utility-scale storage orders are scaling as grids lean on batteries to firm up wind and solar.

Source: Energy-Storage.News

Sodium-ion batteries edge toward the mainstream

CATL's sodium-ion cells are moving from pilot lines to commercial-scale production, with the company signing large supply deals for stationary storage and eyeing EV applications. Sodium-ion trades some energy density for a chemistry free of lithium and cobalt, which could matter most for cheap, safe grid storage rather than long-range vehicles.

Source: Energy Storage (ESS News)

💨 Wind

⚡ China switches on 2 GW offshore wind farm with world's-largest-scale 18 MW turbines

China General Nuclear Power fully commissioned its 2 GW Yangjiang Fanshi offshore project in Guangdong, including 33 turbines rated at 18 MW each — the largest-capacity offshore turbines deployed at scale in China. The project also debuts China's first 500 kV reactive power compensation station for offshore wind and will generate over 6.6 TWh a year, cutting roughly 5.1 million tonnes of CO2 annually.

Source: OffshoreWind.biz

Envision Energy launches next-generation 16.7 MW offshore turbine for Europe

Envision Energy unveiled the EN-252/16.7, a 16.7 MW offshore turbine built on its established Model Z platform and aimed at the European market. The company says the focus is less on raw size and more on squeezing 1–2% more annual energy output and reliability out of each unit after 600+ hours of accelerated life testing.

Source: Ocean Energy Resources

⚡ US House introduces $10 billion fusion investment bill

House leaders introduced the American Leadership in Fusion Act, which would direct $10 billion toward fusion commercialization — including $3.8 billion for new fusion-materials test facilities and $3 billion for a milestone-based demonstration program. Commonwealth Fusion Systems and the Fusion Industry Association backed the bill, a sign fusion is gaining real budget traction in Washington rather than just research funding.

Source: The Fusion Report

nT-Tao and Orion Nuclear Energy to deploy compact fusion for data centers

Israeli fusion firm nT-Tao partnered with US developer Orion Nuclear Energy to deploy compact fusion systems delivering up to 20 MW of continuous power at customer sites, targeting the gap between microreactors and utility-scale SMRs. Data-center power demand is increasingly the customer pulling fusion and advanced nuclear toward commercial deployment.

Source: The Fusion Report

NRC fast-tracks two SMRs at Michigan's Palisades site

The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission fast-tracked construction approval for two small modular reactors at the Palisades Energy Center in Michigan, together rated at 680 MW, allowing early groundbreaking ahead of full licensing. It's a concrete step forward even as analysts continue to question whether first-of-a-kind SMR costs can compete with established plants.

Source: Nuclear-news.net

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ Trane unveils cold-climate heat pump rated down to -29°C

Trane launched its 20 TruComfort Variable Speed Cold Climate Heat Pump with WeatherGuard, available in 3-, 4- and 5-ton models and rated to operate from -29°C to 46°C. The 3-ton unit retains up to 85% of rated heating capacity at -29°C, aimed squarely at northern US states where heat pumps have struggled to displace gas furnaces in the coldest months.

Source: pv magazine

New England's five-state Heat Pump Accelerator keeps rolling

Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire and Rhode Island continue coordinating a $450 million regional Heat Pump Accelerator, aiming for roughly 580,000 installations by 2030, as states pick up momentum lost when federal heat pump tax credits phased out at the end of 2025.
