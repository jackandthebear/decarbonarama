Bottom line

The gap between deployment and politics keeps widening. Solar and storage are compounding at record rates — 43 GW of US utility solar, 72 GW of Chinese solar in six months, 158 GW of global storage forecast this year — while the US Interior Department has now spent about $4bn of public money paying developers to abandon offshore wind. Europe is quietly having a better time of it: He Dreiht's final turbine went in this week, heat pump sales are up 17% and pumped storage has crossed 200 GW globally. Fusion took another $1bn from private investors on the strength of engineering milestones rather than energy output, which tells you where risk appetite currently sits. For UK homeowners the practical news is the simplest: a £9,000 grant, no EPC hurdle, and faster approvals.
