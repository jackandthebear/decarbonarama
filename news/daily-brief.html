Wood Mackenzie analysis reported this month finds sodium-ion supply agreements now exceed 70 GWh, with a claimed ~21% lower 20-year total cost of ownership than LFP despite higher upfront prices. Follow-up coverage this week says the gap with lithium-ion is narrowing as utility-scale storage opportunities grow — a milestone for a chemistry that avoids lithium supply risk.

Grepow launches sodium-ion pouch cells for cold-climate industrial use

Grepow unveiled five sodium-ion pouch cells (2–7 Ah) aimed at industrial equipment, cold-weather power and high-pulse applications, claiming 94% capacity retention after 2,000 cycles versus 83% for a comparable LFP cell. The figures are company tests, but the pouch format is a notable departure from the prismatic cells that dominate sodium-ion volumes.
