☀️ Solar

⚡ Perovskite-silicon tandems cross into commercial production

After LONGi's NREL-certified 34.85% tandem record, perovskite-silicon cells have officially moved from lab records to commercial manufacturing lines. A certified 33.6% flexible tandem also held 91% of its performance after 5,000 bending cycles — opening the door to curved roofs and vehicle-integrated PV.

Source: Interesting Engineering

The catch: warranties still lag silicon

Perovskite's mainstream residential moment is still a 2027-and-beyond story. Warranties currently sit at 10–20 years versus silicon's 25–30, and the lack of 20-year field data keeps traditional project financing cautious until utility-scale results mature over 2027–2029.

Source: Energy Solutions Intelligence

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ First US sodium-ion gigafactory greenlit in Sacramento

Peak Energy will invest up to $71m to build the first sodium-ion battery plant of its kind in the United States, producing up to 4 GWh of grid-storage systems a year. Sodium is roughly a thousand times more abundant than lithium and extractable from seawater — a strong fit for cost-sensitive grid storage.

Source: SodiumBatteryHub

Solid-state sodium battery targets safer, cheaper storage

Researchers report a scalable route to all-solid-state sodium batteries that could improve EV and grid safety while cutting lithium dependence. Meanwhile Changan's Nevo A06, one of the first mass-produced sodium-ion EVs, is expected to reach market later in 2026.

Source: TechXplore

💨 Wind

Sofia and Inch Cape hit major offshore milestones

All 100 Siemens Gamesa SG-14 222 DD turbines are now in place at RWE's 1.4GW Sofia project, while Inch Cape completed installation of all 54 of its 1,080MW monopile foundations. In the Dutch North Sea, the 760MW Hollandse Kust West VI has produced its first power.

Source: Windpower Monthly

Offshore costs now rival gas as France backs 11 projects

New offshore wind is landing at $40–50/MWh LCOE — competitive with new gas and cheaper than new coal. The European Commission has approved France's contracts-for-difference support for 11 offshore projects, and new purpose-built installation vessels are cutting build costs further.

Source: CleanTechnica

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ Fusion funding hits record $4.48bn in a year

The fusion industry raised $4.48bn in the year to July — the highest annual total in the six years the Fusion Industry Association has tracked it. The UK Atomic Energy Authority and Italy's Eni have also formed a joint venture to supply specialist consultancy and operations services to the sector.

Source: ANS Nuclear Newswire

DOE's July 4 criticality deadline focuses the SMR race

Under the DOE Reactor Pilot Program, the goal was for at least three advanced reactors to reach criticality by 4 July 2026 — validating years of design and regulatory work even before power generation. SMR programmes are now tied to specific utilities and sites, with initial operations projected for the early 2030s.

Source: ASME

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ £9,000 heat pump grant launches for off-gas homes

From 21 July, the UK's Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant rises to £9,000 for oil-heated homes in England and Wales — the largest single increase the scheme has offered, targeting 1.7 million off-gas-grid households. Over 200,000 eligible homeowners were leafleted on how to claim.

Source: pv magazine

Record installs, but growth still short of target

The UK hit a record 125,037 heat pump installations in 2025, yet growth slowed to 27% — below the 33% compound rate needed for 450,000/year by 2030. Average air-to-water install costs fell to £13,100 in Q1, 10% cheaper in real terms than 2023, while 0% VAT relief runs to March 2027.

Source: HeatPumpsAndSolar

💧 Hydro

Spain has awarded €165m to seven pumped-hydro projects totalling roughly 2.1GW / 21GWh, six of them new reversible plants. Portugal is targeting 1.4GW of new capacity by 2040 under a fresh National Energy Storage Strategy.

Source: pv magazine

India's Tehri variable-speed scheme nears commissioning

India's 1,000MW Tehri Pumped Storage Project — four 250MW reversible units and the country's first variable-speed installation — is one of the clearest 2026 commissioning cases. In Scotland, Cruachan's unit upgrades are set to be commissioned across 2026–27.

Source: International Water Power

CorPower Ocean has secured a €40m EU Innovation Fund grant to build VianaWave, a 10MW pre-commercial wave farm off northern Portugal. A pipeline of 16 pre-commercial wave and tidal farms — some 196MW and 200 devices — is expected to kick-start the sector's industrial rollout by 2030.

Bottom line

The theme today is technologies crossing from promise into hardware. Perovskite tandems and sodium-ion batteries are moving onto real production lines, offshore wind is now priced against gas, and pumped hydro is winning nine-figure public backing in Spain and India. In the UK, the £9,000 heat pump grant is the sharpest domestic policy lever this month — though record installs still trail the pace net zero demands. Fusion's record $4.48bn haul shows capital conviction remains strong, even as first power stays years out. Cheaper storage and firmer policy are quietly de-risking the grid of the 2030s.
