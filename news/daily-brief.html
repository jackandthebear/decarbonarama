🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ CATL sets out Tener Sodium specs, with overseas deliveries from summer 2027

CATL's sodium-ion grid block delivers 30 MWh+ (about 7.5 MW over four hours), with a claimed 95% round-trip efficiency, 15,000 cycles at 25°C and operation from −35°C to +55°C. In a modelled 500 MWh project it needs 16 blocks versus 23 for its LFP equivalent, with EPC costs estimated 9.2% lower. It's the clearest sign yet that sodium-ion is heading for utility-scale storage.

Source: Shanghai Metals Market

Grepow launches sodium-ion pouch cells for cold-climate industrial use

The Shenzhen firm released five sodium-ion pouch cells (2–7 Ah) aimed at industrial kit and high-pulse cold-weather jobs. Internal tests showed 94% capacity after 2,000 cycles versus 83% for LFP, though those figures are unverified.

Source: ESS News

Sodium-ion closes the gap with lithium-ion

Commercial sodium-ion cells now reach about 175 Wh/kg with strong low-temperature performance, says Turin Polytechnic's Silvia Bodoardo. She sees utility-scale storage as the chemistry's most promising market.

Source: pv magazine USA
