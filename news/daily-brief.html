☀️ Solar

⚡ Chinese lab sets 28.04% world record for perovskite-organic tandem cell

Researchers in China achieved a steady-state conversion efficiency of 28.04% in a perovskite-organic tandem solar cell, a new world record for the architecture. The result strengthens the case for tandem designs that pair perovskites with organic absorbers rather than silicon, potentially enabling lighter and cheaper flexible modules.

Source: Renewables Now

Green River Energy Center comes online: 400 MW solar + 1,600 MWh storage in Utah

rPlus Energies announced the start of operations at its Green River Energy Center in Emery County, Utah, pairing 400 MW of solar with a 1,600 MWh battery. It's one of the largest solar-plus-storage projects to enter service in the US this year, and shows big hybrid plants pressing ahead despite the loss of federal incentives.

Source: Renewable Energy World

Chint and Octopus Energy sign strategic agreements on renewables and VPPs

China's Chint Group signed two cooperation agreements with Octopus Energy covering renewable infrastructure and virtual power plant development in Europe. The tie-up links one of China's biggest electrical equipment makers with Europe's fastest-growing energy retailer, and signals VPPs moving further into the mainstream.

Source: TaiyangNews

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ CATL to deploy 5 GWh of Tener Sodium batteries for grid storage

CATL is lining up 5 GWh of deployments of its Tener Sodium grid-storage platform, betting that sodium-ion's roughly 15,000-cycle life and cold-weather durability will win share from lithium in stationary storage. First deployments are slated for 2027, following the platform's launch in Munich in June.

Source: Electrek

Peak Energy lands up to 4.75 GWh US sodium-ion supply deal with Jupiter Power

US startup Peak Energy will supply up to 4.75 GWh of sodium-ion batteries to Jupiter Power through 2030 — a deal worth up to $500 million and the largest announced US sodium-ion deployment to date. A separate pilot with RWE near Milwaukee would put the first sodium-ion system on the MISO grid.

Source: IEEE Spectrum

Matrix Renewables closes £245m financing for 1 GWh Scottish battery project

Matrix Renewables closed a £245 million non-recourse project financing for a 500 MW / 1,000 MWh battery energy storage system in southern Scotland. It's another sign that UK grid-scale storage has matured into a bankable, institutional asset class.

Source: Renewable Energy World

💨 Wind

Ørsted completes turbine installation at 920 MW Greater Changhua 2b & 4

All 66 of the 14 MW Siemens Gamesa turbines are now installed at Ørsted's Greater Changhua 2b and 4 offshore wind farms in Taiwan. The 920 MW build-out is a landmark for Asia-Pacific offshore wind as the region takes up the slack from a retreating US market.

Source: Ørsted

RWE installs world-first turbine with CO₂-reduced steel tower and recyclable blades

At the 1.1 GW Thor project off Denmark's west coast, RWE installed the first offshore turbine built with a CO₂-reduced steel tower and fully recyclable rotor blades. It's a meaningful step in cutting the embodied carbon of wind hardware itself.

Source: RWE

US offshore projects report progress after courts halt stop-work orders

Three of five offshore wind projects under construction in the northeast US report solid progress after winning preliminary injunctions against federal stop-work orders — Sunrise Wind is now roughly 45% complete, with 44 of 84 monopiles and its offshore converter station installed.

Source: The Maritime Executive

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant rises to £9,000 for oil and LPG homes

From 21 July, the UK's Boiler Upgrade Scheme pays £9,000 (up from £7,500) toward a heat pump for homes currently heated by oil or LPG — around 1.7 million households. It's the largest single grant increase in the scheme's history and squarely targets off-gas-grid homes, where heat pump economics are strongest.

Source: Heat Pumps & Solar

Heat Pump Association urges stronger policy for heat pump-driven heat networks

The HPA published recommendations calling for regulatory clarity, targeted funding and policy recognition for low-carbon heat networks powered by heat pumps. Heat networks are expected to serve around 20% of UK heat demand by 2050, up from roughly 3% today.

Source: Heat Pump Association

Mitsubishi Electric makes the case for high-temperature heat pumps in retrofit

A new Mitsubishi Electric white paper argues next-generation high-temperature heat pumps can decarbonise home heating with less cost and disruption, by working with existing radiators — a key lever for hitting the UK's 450,000 installations-a-year target by 2030.

Source: BSEE

💧 Hydro

⚡ Britain approves first new pumped storage projects in over 40 years

Three major pumped storage hydropower schemes in Scotland have won provisional approval — the first new UK pumped hydro in more than four decades. The "water batteries" will store surplus wind power and strengthen energy security as the grid decarbonises.

Source: No2NuclearPower news digest

Spain awards €165m to seven pumped hydro projects totalling 21 GWh

Spain's government awarded €165 million to seven reversible pumped hydro projects adding 2.07 GW of capacity and over 21 GWh of storage across six regions, deepening its long-duration storage push behind a heavily solar grid.

Source: pv magazine

Global pumped storage capacity passes 200 GW after record year

The IHA's World Hydropower Outlook reports global pumped storage has surpassed 200 GW following a record year for new capacity — cementing its role as the world's largest form of energy storage by far.

Source: International Hydropower Association

🌊 Wave & Tidal

⚡ IRENA's flagship cost report covers ocean energy for the first time

IRENA's Renewable Power Generation Costs report includes a dedicated ocean energy assessment for the first time, finding wave and tidal on a credible path to commercial scale: a 196 MW pipeline of 16 pre-commercial farms by 2030, with costs projected to fall to ~$120/MWh (wave) and ~$140/MWh (tidal) at 2 GW deployed.

Source: Ocean News & Technology

Ocean Energy Europe: wave and tidal could supply 13% of global electricity

The first global assessment of wave and tidal resources, published by Ocean Energy Europe, finds ocean energy could meet 13% of world electricity demand and 21% of current EU consumption — framing it as the largest untapped renewable resource.

Source: ECO Magazine

Bottom line

Sodium-ion is the story of the moment: CATL, Peak Energy and Jupiter Power are turning it from lab curiosity into gigawatt-hour-scale, bankable grid storage. It's also a strong stretch for the UK — heat pump grants jump to £9,000 for oil-heated homes, Scotland gets its first new pumped hydro approvals in 40 years, and a private consortium is chasing the UK's first commercial fusion plant. Offshore wind keeps advancing everywhere from Taiwan to Denmark even as US federal headwinds persist, and ocean energy just earned its first serious cost validation from IRENA. The direction of travel: storage in all its forms — electrochemical, pumped, thermal — is now the centre of gravity of the transition.
