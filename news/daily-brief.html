☀️ Solar

⚡ LONGi sets 35.5% tandem-cell world record

LONGi's crystalline silicon-perovskite tandem cell hit 35.5% conversion efficiency, certified by Europe's ESTI — a fresh world record for the mainstream next-gen route, which has a theoretical ceiling near 43%. Higher efficiency means fewer panels for the same output, improving returns for both rooftop and utility projects.

Source: CleanTechnica

Lab records still outrun the shop floor

Commercial perovskite modules in 2026 remain in the 18–22% band, and full utility-scale deployment isn't expected until 2027–2029 once 20-year field data exists to satisfy lenders. The gap underlines how record headlines translate slowly into installed capacity.

Source: Energy Solutions Intelligence

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ Peak plans first US sodium-ion gigafactory

Peak Energy will invest up to $71m in a Sacramento plant — the first sodium-ion manufacturing facility of its kind in the US — producing up to 4 GWh of grid storage systems a year. Sodium sidesteps lithium supply constraints and offers better safety for stationary storage.

Source: SodiumBatteryHub

Oxford, Nissan and Gelion chase solid-state lithium-sulfur

A new three-year CoRe-SoLiS project brings together Gelion, Nissan Technical Centre Europe and the University of Oxford to develop safer, higher-density solid-state lithium-sulfur cells for EVs and grids. Solid-state remains ~1% of funded battery capacity today, so early R&D partnerships matter.

Source: Live Science

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ Fusion funding hits record $4.48bn

The Fusion Industry Association reports $4.48bn raised in the year to July — the highest annual total in the six years it has tracked the figure. The US NRC has also formally separated fusion from fission regulation, focusing rules on tritium and activation products rather than fission-style cooling systems.

Source: World Nuclear News

DOE eyes July 4 criticality milestone for advanced reactors

Under the DOE's Reactor Pilot Program, the goal is for at least three advanced reactors to reach criticality — a self-sustaining chain reaction — by 4 July 2026. It doesn't mean power on the grid, but it validates years of design and regulatory work toward commercialisation.

Source: Yahoo Finance

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ £9,000 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant goes live

From 21 July the UK's Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant rose 20% — from £7,500 to £9,000 — for oil-heated homes in England and Wales. It's the largest single grant increase in the scheme's history, aimed at the 1.7 million off-gas-grid households.

Source: Installer Online

Adoption grows, but below the 2030 trajectory

UK heat pump installs hit a record 125,037 in 2025, yet 27% growth trails the ~33% compound rate needed to reach 450,000 a year by 2030. The Clean Heat Market Mechanism now requires boiler makers to sell heat pumps equal to 8% of boiler sales or face penalties, while average install costs have fallen ~10% since 2023.

Source: HeatPumpsAndSolar

💧 Hydro

⚡ Spain awards €165m for 2.1 GW of pumped storage

Spain granted €165m to seven pumped hydro projects totalling ~2.1 GW / 21 GWh, six of them new reversible plants; Portugal is targeting 1.4 GW of new capacity by 2040 under a fresh national storage strategy. Long-duration pumped hydro is re-emerging as the workhorse for balancing high-renewables grids.

Source: pv magazine

World Bank backs Morocco's Ifahsa pumped storage

The World Bank approved $265m for the Ifahsa Pumped Hydropower Storage Project in northern Morocco — one of the most significant clean-storage investments of its kind on the African continent. India, meanwhile, has ~15.9 GW of pumped storage under construction.

Source: World Bank

🌊 Wave & Tidal

CorPower Ocean secured a €40m EU Innovation Fund grant to build VianaWave, a 10 MW pre-commercial wave farm off northern Portugal. IRENA's latest report sees costs falling to $120–140/MWh at 2 GW scale, with a pipeline of ~16 pre-commercial farms (~196 MW) expected by 2030.

Source: reNews

California maps out wave and tidal deployment zones

The California Energy Commission published its final SB 605 report assessing suitable sea areas, grid infrastructure and commercialisation pathways for wave and tidal energy, citing Eco Wave Power's Port of L.A. pilot. It's a step toward a formal state framework for marine energy.

In short: strong momentum in R&D and storage, with policy support doing the heavy lifting on deployment.
