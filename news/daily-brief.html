☀️ Solar

⚡ Five countries, one week: over a gigawatt of new solar commitments

Between 20 and 21 August, developers and financiers in the US, Egypt, Paraguay, India and Australia advanced projects totalling well over a gigawatt of solar capacity plus hundreds of millions of dollars in fresh financing. It is a snapshot of how routine GW-scale solar dealmaking has become — the building season is now genuinely global rather than concentrated in one or two markets.

Sol Systems acquired the 200MWac Lumberton Solar Project in Hardin County, Texas from DESRI and will take it through to construction. Solar-plus-storage as the default configuration continues to harden.

US on track for a record 43.4GW of utility-scale solar this year

The EIA still expects developers to add 43.4GW of new utility-scale solar PV in 2026, a roughly 60% jump on 2025 and the largest single-year build in US history. Solar and storage together are forecast to account for the bulk of an 86GW capacity surge — a useful reality check against the narrative that US clean energy has stalled.

Perovskite tandems hold above 34% as commercial shipments creep forward

LONGi's certified 34.85% perovskite-silicon tandem cell remains the benchmark, with NREL certifying single-junction perovskite at 27.3%. Commercial tandem modules are shipping in small volumes but are still nowhere near distributor shelves — the gap between lab record and buyable product remains the sector's defining problem.

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ CATL says sodium-ion will hit cost parity with LFP by year-end

CATL has told customers its sodium-ion cells should match lithium iron phosphate on cost by the end of 2026, while retaining over 90% of capacity at −20°C and delivering roughly three times LFP's discharge power at −30°C. If parity lands on schedule, cold-climate grid storage and backup applications get a cheaper, lithium-free option almost overnight.

Source: Sodium Battery Hub

Boralex and Six Nations close US$145m on a 500MWh Ontario battery

Boralex and the Six Nations of the Grand River Development Corporation secured CA$202m (about US$145m) of financing for a 125MW/500MWh BESS in Oxford County, Ontario. The Indigenous co-ownership structure is becoming a standard feature of Canadian storage financing rather than an exception.

Source: Energy-Storage.News

GE Vernova and CATL picked for 1,216MWh Supernode Stage 3 in Queensland

The third stage of Supernode will extend what is already Australia's largest battery storage system by a further 1,216MWh. Queensland is using storage to absorb an increasingly solar-heavy midday grid, and the repeat orders suggest the economics are holding up as more capacity crowds in.

Source: Energy-Storage.News

Solid-state sodium chemistry targets the safety problem in grid storage

Most sodium-ion cells still use flammable liquid electrolytes — a real concern at multi-hundred-megawatt-hour scale. Researchers have demonstrated an all-solid-state sodium battery aimed squarely at cheap, long-lived, non-flammable stationary storage. Still lab-stage, but it addresses the objection that most often stalls large BESS planning applications.

Source: TechXplore

24% of new-build homes in England are being fitted with a heat pump, and in Wales the figure has stayed above 30% for three straight months. New build is the easy win — well-insulated fabric, no retrofit disruption — but it also quietly builds the installer base the retrofit market needs.

Since 21 July 2026, eligible off-gas-grid properties in England and Wales heated by oil or LPG can claim £9,000 towards an air or ground source heat pump, up from the standard £7,500 that remains available to everyone else.
