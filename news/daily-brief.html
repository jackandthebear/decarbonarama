☀️ Solar

US solar module prices jump over 40% after Section 232 tariffs

Anza data shows imported module prices in the US rose from $0.27/W to $0.38/W following August's Section 232 tariffs, with TOPCon averaging $0.38/W. It raises project costs for developers just as new polysilicon import limits take effect ahead of tariffs starting 4 December.

⚡ Abu Dhabi lifts solar target to 14GW by 2030

Emirates Water and Electricity raised its 2030 solar goal from 10GW to 14GW and set 35GW+ for 2035, backed by 15GW of battery storage. The plan is designed to cut the emirate's reliance on gas-fired generation.

Source: PV Tech

Court reinstates the $7bn Solar for All programme

A federal judge ruled the EPA unlawfully terminated the Solar for All programme and vacated the termination. The ruling revives funding aimed at bringing rooftop and community solar to low-income households.

The world added 693GW of renewables in 2025, reaching 5.15TW cumulative capacity. IRENA says solar PV growth makes the higher pace feasible.
