⚡ US battery installations hit a record 20.2 GWh in a single quarter

New utility-scale and behind-the-meter battery capacity added in Q2 2026 could power roughly 700,000 homes daily. Seven gigascale systems came online in the quarter, and 2026 installations are projected to reach 71 GWh — though residential battery uptake is set to dip 16% after a tax credit expired.

Utilities plan 24 GW of new battery storage for 2026 as interconnection queues stretch

The EIA reports utilities plan roughly 24 GW of new utility-scale battery storage this year, on top of more than 40 GW installed over the past five years. The binding constraint is no longer equipment supply but how long it takes to get grid interconnection approved.

Source: GlobeNewswire

⚡ Revolution Wind finishes installing all 65 turbines

The $6.2 billion offshore project off Rhode Island and Connecticut has completed turbine installation and will reach its full 704 MW capacity by year-end. The project survived two Trump administration stop-work orders, both overturned by federal courts, and has already supplied power during winter storms and summer heat waves.

The nation's largest offshore wind project has seen costs climb roughly $300 million to about $11.7 billion, with tariffs and revised grid-upgrade charges cited as drivers. Completion has slipped six months to the end of 2027, though 31 of 176 turbines are already installed.

💧 Hydro

Himalayan glacier collapse buries a dozen hydropower plants in Nepal

An August 26 glacier and bedrock collapse in Nepal's Rasuwa district sent 30-foot waves of water, boulders and ice down the Trishuli River, burying 12 hydropower plants including the World Bank-backed Upper Trishuli-1 project. Roughly 1,400 bodies have been recovered and over 5,000 people remain unaccounted for; experts are questioning whether cumulative risk across the basin's 36 hydropower projects was properly assessed.

Source: OPB
