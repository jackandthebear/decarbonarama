☀️ Solar

⚡ Perovskite triple-junction cell hits 27.3% with record 770-hour stability

A team at Helmholtz-Zentrum Berlin has built a triple-junction cell stacking three different perovskite semiconductors, using a novel graphene-oxide plus self-assembled-monolayer bilayer as the hole conductor. Efficiency held at 27.3% with almost no decline after 770 hours of operation — stability, not peak efficiency, is the barrier perovskites have to clear before they can be sold with a 25-year warranty.

Source: TechXplore

⚡ Chinese perovskite-organic tandem sets 28.04% world record

The Institute of Chemistry at the Chinese Academy of Sciences reported a steady-state 28.04% conversion efficiency for a perovskite-organic tandem cell, a world record for the architecture. The device retained 90% of its initial output after 625 hours of continuous illumination.

Source: pv magazine

US on track for a record 43.4 GW of utility-scale solar this year

EIA projections have developers adding 43.4 GW of utility-scale solar PV in 2026, a 60% jump on the record 27.2 GW installed in 2025. Texas alone accounts for roughly 40% of the planned build at 17.4 GW.

Source: PV Tech

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ CATL deploys 5 GWh of sodium-ion for grid storage

CATL is rolling out 5 GWh of its Tener sodium-ion product, pitching a 15,000-cycle life and wide-temperature tolerance as a better fit for stationary storage than lithium. It follows a 60 GWh sodium supply deal with integrator HyperStrong in April — the largest sodium order placed to date.

Source: Electrek

⚡ Peak Energy to build first US sodium-ion gigafactory in Sacramento

Peak Energy Technologies will invest up to $71 million in a Sacramento plant producing up to 4 GWh a year of grid-scale sodium-ion systems — the first facility of its kind in the United States. It is a meaningful test of whether sodium supply chains can be localised outside China.

Source: Sodium Battery Hub

Reality check: sodium and solid-state still tiny slices of capacity

Analysis this month puts solid-state at about 1% and sodium at about 4% of existing or funded global battery manufacturing capacity. Grid storage prizes cycle life, safety and cost over energy density, which is why sodium's share is expected to grow well before it does in cars.

Source: Yale E360

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant rose from £7,500 to £9,000 for homes in England and Wales currently heated by oil or LPG — roughly 1.7 million households. Off-gas-grid properties have the strongest running-cost case for a heat pump, so targeting them is the most efficient use of the subsidy pot.

Bottom line

Today's theme is durability over headline numbers. The two perovskite records that matter this week were flagged as much for their 625- and 770-hour stability runs as for their 27–28% efficiencies, because stability is what stands between lab cells and bankable modules. In storage, sodium-ion is quietly winning the grid argument on cycle life and cost rather than energy density — CATL's 5 GWh rollout and the first US sodium gigafactory are the same bet placed from opposite ends of the supply chain. Spain's €165m pumped hydro award and the 200 GW global milestone are a reminder that the largest storage asset class is still made of water and gravity. And for UK homeowners, the practical news is the £9,000 grant that went live on 21 July for the 1.7 million households burning oil or LPG.
