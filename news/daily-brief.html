☀️ Solar

⚡ China proposes export controls on solar-manufacturing technologies

Beijing has put forward curbs on exports of certain solar-manufacturing technologies, reported by Bloomberg and Reuters/US News on 16–18 October, as the US and India push to build up their own manufacturing. If adopted, the controls could slow overseas factory build-outs and add upward pressure on module prices.

Source: US News / Reuters

🔋 Battery Technology

Fluence to supply 1 GWh battery for Vattenfall in Germany

Fluence has been selected to supply a roughly 1 GWh battery storage system for Vattenfall in Germany, reported on 15 October. Large grid batteries like this help absorb surplus wind and solar output and firm up the German grid.

Source: Energy-Storage.News (ESS News)

💨 Wind

Floating offshore wind: a quarter-scale turbine off Maine faces a political test

Floating turbines have advanced through international collaboration and a quarter-scale demonstration now operating off Maine. But project delays and US federal actions against offshore wind, including halted projects and cancelled port grants, put the sector’s future there in doubt, with Europe and Canada potentially pulling ahead.

Source: Inside Climate News

⚛️ Nuclear

Nuclear uprates: squeezing more power from existing reactors

Operators are boosting output at existing reactors through equipment upgrades and extra fuel, driven by AI-related demand and deals with Big Tech. US uprate potential is roughly 3–8 GW, a small share of the ~30 GW of extra peak demand PJM expects from AI by 2030, so new reactors will likely be needed eventually.

Source: MIT Technology Review

♨️ Heat Pumps

Canada launches $13M Newfoundland heat pump programme ahead of national rebate

Federal Environment Minister Julie Dabrusin announced a $13 million Take Charge NL programme to help residents fund heat pumps and insulation, with Ottawa’s $2B National Heat Pump Rebate arriving this winter. Installers who had been idle since the previous programme ended say they are ready for the surge, though the oil-to-electric funding may run out as early as January.

Source: VOCM

💧 Hydro

Nepal’s 30 MW Khani Khola hydropower begins test generation

The Dolakha district plant began test generation on 4 October and is feeding the national grid, with commercial operation due after a 15-day test period. Construction was delayed by flooding in July 2024 that damaged the dam site.

Source: Kathmandu Post

🌊 Wave & Tidal

⚡ Orbital’s four-turbine EURO-TIDES array cleared for construction in Orkney

The European Commission has approved the project at the European Marine Energy Centre, with deployment expected in 2028 once Orbital completes its current investment round. The array could power about 9,200 homes and marks a shift from single-device testing to multi-turbine tidal farms.

Source: Offshore Energy

Minesto and Trayen Power sign MoU for tidal and ocean-current projects in Chile

The partners will scout sites for Minesto’s Dragon Class technology, run feasibility studies and seek project funding, giving Minesto a foothold in a market it rates highly.

Source: Offshore Energy
