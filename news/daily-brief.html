🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ CATL and HyperStrong sign a 60 GWh sodium-ion storage deal

The three-year agreement is the largest sodium-ion storage supply contract announced anywhere to date. Sodium-ion trades energy density for cost, thermal stability and cycle life — exactly the trade-off grid storage wants — and a contract at this scale signals the chemistry has left the pilot phase.

Source: MIT Technology Review

Sodium-ion reaches mass-market EVs

Changan is preparing the Nevo A06, among the first mass-produced EVs running a sodium-ion pack, for market later this year. Grid-scale pilots are also live in California and New York. If sodium works in a car, the supply chain scales far faster than a storage-only market would allow.

Source: CleanTechnica

Solid-state still under 1% of global battery capacity

Despite persistent headlines, solid-state accounts for roughly 1% of existing or funded global battery manufacturing capacity. The more interesting near-term work is at the intersection — sodium all-solid-state systems promising cheap, safe, long-lived storage without lithium supply exposure.

Source: Yale E360

♨️ Heat Pumps

Solar-plus-storage heat pumps cut grid draw by ~60%

New modelling published this month finds heat pumps paired with rooftop PV and a battery reduce grid electricity use by 59.89% versus a heat pump alone. That's the strongest argument yet for treating solar, storage and heating as one retrofit rather than three separate purchases.

Source: pv magazine

UK deployment on course for a record year — but the spark gap bites

More than 125,000 heat pumps were sold in the UK in 2025, with installations up 11% year on year, and the Boiler Upgrade Scheme's £7,500 grant is now well established. The Future Homes Standard bans gas boilers in most new homes from 2027, implying 100,000–300,000 units a year from developers alone. The electricity-to-gas price gap remains the main brake on voluntary switching.

Source: Elite Renewables

Shenling launches new air-to-water range; Carrier trials battery-enabled HVAC

Shenling released an all-in-one air-to-water series at 8.03, 9.88 and 11.75 kW. Separately, Carrier has begun the largest field trial in its 123-year history, testing battery-enabled HVAC that can store and shift power on grid signal — heat pumps as a flexibility asset, not just a load.

Source: pv magazine

💧 Hydro

⚡ Global pumped storage passes 200 GW for the first time

The IHA's World Hydropower Outlook reports 28 GW of new hydro capacity in 2025 — including a record 11.6 GW of pumped storage — taking global installed hydro to 1,469 GW and pumped storage past the 200 GW mark. The development pipeline stands at 1,127 GW, of which 621 GW is pumped storage.

Source: International Hydropower Association

China holds 218 GW of pumped storage under construction

China accounted for over 40% of global hydro additions in 2025 and now has more than 300 GW under construction, 218 GW of it pumped storage. The Shangyi station in Hebei has closed its lower reservoir for filling and installed the Unit 1 rotor.

Source: Power Engineering

Alpine hydro near record lows as European drought worsens

Hydropower output across the Alpine region is close to an all-time low amid a severe summer drought, with heatwaves pushing spot electricity prices toward €500/MWh in places. A pointed reminder that hydro is a climate-exposed asset as well as a climate solution.

Source: CleanTechnica

Bottom line

The technology story keeps outrunning the politics. Sodium-ion has gone from a lab hedge to a 60 GWh supply contract and a mass-market EV in under two years, pumped storage has quietly passed 200 GW worldwide, and fusion just absorbed another billion dollars of private equity. Against that, Washington paid RWE $1.22 billion this week to not build offshore wind — the fifth such deal — while Europe's Alpine hydro sits near record lows in a drought that is itself a symptom of the problem. For UK homeowners the practical signal is the one from the modelling desk: heat pumps paired with solar and a battery cut grid electricity draw by roughly 60%, and with the Future Homes Standard landing in 2027 the question is shifting from whether to electrify heating to how cheaply you can run it once you have.
