Sungrow's PowerTitan 3.0 survives a 7.5-hour large-scale fire test

In a UL Solutions test on a 28.56 MWh installation, a fire inside one container peaked near 1,260°C but did not spread to neighbouring units. Fire propagation is a key barrier to siting grid batteries, so passing such tests matters for permitting and insurance.
