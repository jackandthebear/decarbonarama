Bottom line

Two records landed in the same week from opposite ends of the maturity curve: solar crossed 3 terawatts globally, and pumped storage crossed 200 GW — the oldest storage technology and the newest generation technology both compounding faster than forecast. Storage is the through-line everywhere today, from Egypt's Obelisk hybrid to a 1 GW long-duration JV built specifically for AI data centres, and increasingly it is being procured as infrastructure rather than piloted as innovation. The US court decision unfreezing wind permitting removes the sector's biggest single blocker, though developers will want proof it sticks before recommitting capital. The uncomfortable outlier remains UK heat pumps: grant demand is up 19% and Octopus just had its best month ever, yet total installations are down 22% year to date — a market where the subsidy works and the running-cost economics still do not. Fix the electricity-to-gas price ratio and that number inverts overnight.
