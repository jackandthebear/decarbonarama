☀️ Solar

⚡ Full-size perovskite modules hit record 19.6% on a mass-production line

UtmoLight and UNSW Sydney recorded 19.6% efficiency on standard-size 2.81 m² perovskite modules, up from the previous 17.44% benchmark. The figure came off UtmoLight's GW-scale production line rather than a lab bench, a meaningful signal that perovskites are moving towards commercial manufacturing.

Source: Now.Solar / Yahoo Tech

⚡ JinkoSolar claims 35.24% for perovskite/TOPCon tandem cell

JinkoSolar says its N-type TOPCon-based perovskite tandem cell exceeded 35.24% efficiency, certified by the Chinese Academy of Sciences' Shanghai institute. It beats the company's own 34.82% from five months ago and shows how fast tandem cells are pulling ahead of single-junction silicon.

Source: Now.Solar / Green Building Africa

Finnish partners line up 461 MW of utility-scale solar

Solarigo, Wisol and Ensto Maviko signed a letter of intent to build 461 MW of solar in Finland between 2027 and 2029. The same roundup notes Next2Sun Austria's insolvency filing, a reminder that Europe's solar sector remains under margin pressure even as Nordic build-out grows.

Source: TaiyangNews

🔋 Battery Technology

L&T wins 300 MW / 900 MWh battery order at Khavda, Gujarat

Larsen & Toubro will build a liquid-cooled, three-hour battery system at Khavda, home to one of the world's largest renewable parks. Large-scale storage like this is becoming essential for India to absorb its surging solar and wind output.

Source: SolarQuarter

⚡ Egypt sets 2030 targets: 31.9 GW renewables and 14.4 GWh of batteries

Egypt's government says it plans 31,905 MW of renewable capacity and 14,420 MWh of battery storage by 2030, reviewed by PM Mostafa Madbouly and the energy minister. Delivery depends heavily on private and partner projects, but the storage target is one of the largest in Africa.

Source: SolarQuarter

Ivory Coast ratifies World Bank funding for 205 MWh of grid batteries

The Council of Ministers approved about €5.2 million in additional IDA funding for a regional power project that includes a 205 MWh battery system, 105 MWh of it for northern towns Korhogo, Boundiali and Odienné. It is a small but notable step for grid storage in West Africa.

Source: The Rio Times

💨 Wind

⚡ All 62 turbines installed at France's 500 MW Dieppe-Le Tréport farm

Ocean Winds completed turbine installation at the Dieppe-Le Tréport offshore farm after six months of work by Jan De Nul's Vole au Vent, with full power expected within weeks. The 62 Siemens Gamesa 8.4 MW machines will supply roughly 850,000 people.

Source: offshoreWIND.biz

Philippines breaks ground on its first offshore wind port

The Philippine Ports Authority began work on the P2.3-billion Pambuhan Offshore Wind Port in Camarines Norte, a staging hub for turbine components serving the Bicol Region's waters. It's a key piece of logistics for the country's 35%-renewables-by-2030 goal.

Source: Daily Tribune

UK offshore wind and oil & gas decommissioning will compete for vessels and workers

OEUK's 2026 Decommissioning Report projects a ~33.7 GW UK offshore wind pipeline to 2035, peaking at 5.2 GW of additions in 2029, alongside £24.7bn of decommissioning spend. It warns of clashes over ports, vessels and skilled labour and urges earlier contracting.

Source: offshoreWIND.biz

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ US Congress moves to let the NRC skip uncontested reactor hearings

Both chambers have passed language letting the NRC skip mandatory permit hearings unless one is requested, the House by voice vote on 11 November and the Senate within a broader permitting bill on 19 November. Because it covers operating licences too, it could shave time off projects like Dow/X-energy's Seadrift, Texas plant, permitted on 9 November.

Source: Daily Caller

America's oldest reactor, Nine Mile Point 1, clears environmental review for 80-year life

The NRC issued a Finding of No Significant Impact for Constellation's plan to run the 613 MWe Oswego County, New York unit, online since 1969, through August 2049. A final licensing decision is targeted for April 2027.

Source: CASRAI

Fusion roundup: tritium licence for Pacific Fusion, $126m for Japan's Helical Fusion

Pacific Fusion received New Mexico's first radioactive-materials licence for a private fusion firm, covering tritium at its $1bn Albuquerque campus, while Japan's METI awarded Helical Fusion up to ¥19.2bn for its Helix HARUKA device. TAE is targeting 100 million °C in its upgraded Norm reactor in 2027.

Source: The Fusion Report

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ UK heat pump grant applications hit a record — in the month future funding was cut

Government figures show more than 14,200 Boiler Upgrade Scheme applications in October, around 5.2 times the long-term average, after the grant was raised for oil-heated, off-gas homes. The ECIU warns that Budget cuts to future years' funding could see vouchers run out just as demand peaks.

Source: ECIU

💧 Hydro

Kyrgyzstan has commissioned ~50 hydropower plants in five years, with 50 more under way

Energy Minister Altynbek Rysbekov told a Belt and Road energy meeting in Qingdao that around 50 small and medium plants have come online, with a similar number in construction. He also cited a 240 MW gain from modernising Toktogul and progress on the 1,860 MW Kambarata-1 project.

Source: Trend News Agency

COP31 white paper calls pumped storage "strategic national security infrastructure"

The International Hydropower Association and the Global Alliance for Pumped Storage launched a white paper with input from 60+ governments, setting out seven policy recommendations to speed investment in "water batteries". It ties into a COP31 pledge to lift electricity's share of final energy from about 23% to 35% by 2035.

Source: International Hydropower Association

🌊 Wave & Tidal

Eco Wave Power gets letter of intent for up to 100 MW of wave energy in Africa

An unnamed African utility signed a non-binding LOI with Eco Wave Power's US subsidiary to explore up to 100 MW of wave capacity. It builds on a February study identifying about 8.3 MW of potential at South Africa's Port of Ngqura breakwater, though it is not yet a firm deal.

Source: Renewable Energy Magazine

1 MW floating wave converter licensed for Orkney test site

The €19.6m WEDUSEA project won a Scottish marine licence to install OceanEnergy's 1 MW OE Buoy at EMEC's Billia Croo site, exporting power via subsea cable. It's one of the larger wave devices heading for open-sea testing in Europe.

Source: Offshore Energy
