Bottom line

Today's stories share a theme: the clean-energy build-out is increasingly being paced by things that are not the generating technology itself. Cadeler is ordering installation vessels for 2031, GE Vernova is refurbishing a 1984 pumped-storage plant, OEG is selling corrosion protection, and Energy Vault's biggest new customer is a data centre. Meanwhile the two genuinely new technologies in the brief — perovskite tandems above 34% and CorPower's DNV-certified wave device — both hit milestones about bankability rather than raw performance. In the UK, EDF's seven-year warranty and the £9,000 oil-to-heat-pump grant are aimed squarely at consumer confidence, which Q1's 10,693 installations suggest is still the bottleneck. The technology is mostly solved; the supply chains, balance sheets and household trust are what remain.
