The NRC approved a construction permit for four Xe-100 high-temperature gas reactors at Dow's Seadrift site — the first advanced reactor approved for a US industrial site and the first gas-reactor permit in nearly 60 years. The result, published in Physical Review Letters, is early-stage but addresses one of the key engineering barriers to fusion power plants.
