☀️ Solar

Masdar and Terra-Gen complete $950m Lockhart solar-plus-storage complex in California

The Lockhart project is now online with 365 MW of solar and 173.7 MW of battery storage feeding the California grid. It is another sign that new US solar is increasingly being built hybrid, with storage attached from day one to shift output into the evening peak.

Source: pv magazine USA

T1 Energy pushes Austin solar cell fab ramp to mid-2027

T1 Energy reported $197 million in Q3 net sales but delayed completion of its Texas cell factory to mid-2027. The slip shows how hard it still is to onshore the cell step of the US solar supply chain, as opposed to module assembly alone.

Source: pv magazine USA

⚡ Connecticut utilities must now offer flexible interconnection to solar projects

A Connecticut solar trade group is claiming a win after regulators required utilities to offer flexible interconnection. That lets projects connect sooner by agreeing to curtail output at grid-constrained times instead of waiting for costly upgrades, which tackles one of the biggest bottlenecks for distributed solar.

Source: pv magazine USA

HY SOLAR ships first modules to 600 MW Inner Mongolia project

HY SOLAR has delivered its first modules to a 600 MW project in Ordos (about 723 MWp, bid at RMB 0.698/W). Separately, Qinghai is targeting 8 GW of concentrated solar power by 2030, which points to Chinese interest in solar that can dispatch on demand.

Source: TaiyangNews

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ BNEF forecasts 209 GW of new batteries in 2027

BloombergNEF expects 209 GW of new battery storage to be deployed globally in 2027, and about 3 TW / 11 TWh cumulative by 2036. Storage is quickly becoming the main way grids firm up cheap solar and wind.

Source: pv magazine USA

NYSERDA launches 1 GW energy storage solicitation

New York's energy agency has opened a solicitation for 1 GW of energy storage. The procurement moves the state closer to its bulk storage targets as it tries to replace peaker plants and integrate more offshore wind and solar.

Source: TaiyangNews

Sodium-ion closes the gap: Grepow launches cold-climate pouch cells

Grepow has released five sodium-ion pouch cells (2–7 Ah, 3.0 V) aimed at industrial and cold-weather uses. In company tests they kept 94% of capacity after 2,000 cycles, against 83% for an LFP cell (not independently verified). This comes as commercial sodium-ion reaches about 175 Wh/kg and CATL pushes its Tener sodium storage platform.

Source: ESS News

💨 Wind

⚡ Vineyard Wind and Revolution Wind both reach full operation

Vineyard Wind 1 (62 turbines, 800 MW, enough for about 400,000 homes) is now fully commercial off Massachusetts. Revolution Wind (704 MW) reached full power the same week. That gives New England about 1.5 GW of operating offshore wind despite federal headwinds.

Source: WBUR

About 400 US onshore wind projects stuck awaiting Pentagon review

A Defense Department official told a court that about 400 onshore wind projects are queued for DOD review, with action resting on just eight federal employees. Plaintiffs say reviews have not really resumed, and their reply is due 17 November. This is a key bottleneck for US onshore wind.

Source: Utility Dive

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ New York Power Authority qualifies 10 developers for upstate advanced nuclear

NYPA has qualified 10 developers, including NextEra, Holtec, TerraPower, X-energy and The Nuclear Company, as potential partners for at least 1 GW of new nuclear in upstate New York. This is a concrete step toward the state's 5 GW nuclear goal and one of the first state-led new-build processes in the US.

Source: Democrat and Chronicle via Yahoo News

⚡ UK invites £115m in bids to build STEP prototype fusion plant

UK Fusion Energy is tendering five engineering lots, worth up to £115 million, for the STEP plant at West Burton, covering the breeder blanket, fuel cycle, test operations, controls and systems engineering. Bids close 29 January 2027 for a plant targeted to operate by 2040.

Source: World Nuclear News

IAEA warns of nuclear safety risks as strikes hit Ukraine's grid

Backup diesel generators started at two Ukrainian nuclear sites, including Chernobyl, after grid dips. Zaporizhzhia lost all off-site power for more than 12 hours, its 27th such loss since the full-scale war began. IAEA chief Rafael Grossi warned that grid risk to nuclear safety will rise through the winter.

Source: Newsweek

♨️ Heat Pumps

Heat pumps could cut a quarter of EU energy emissions, says EHPA

The European Heat Pump Association estimates that a full rollout could avoid about 600 Mt of CO2, roughly 25% of EU energy-sector emissions. Europe has about 30 million residential units, with about 3 million added in 2025, against a Commission ambition of 4 million installs a year.

Source: Renewable Energy Magazine

Rinnai launches new residential heat pump water heaters

Rinnai's new REHPW range comes in 189, 246 and 303 litre sizes, with a UEF of up to 4.0. It runs in heat pump mode down to about 2.8°C. Heat pump water heaters are a fast-growing, lower-cost entry point to household electrification.

Source: pv magazine

Bosch unveils residential air-to-air heat pumps

Bosch has introduced a new line of residential air-to-air heat pumps, which give both heating and cooling from one unit. It is another major manufacturer widening its range as demand builds in warm-climate markets.

Source: pv magazine India

💧 Hydro

⚡ COP31 white paper urges pumped-storage reforms

The white paper was launched in Antalya with input from more than 60 governments through the Global Alliance for Pumped Storage. It sets out seven policy recommendations to unlock "water batteries", framed around the COP31 pledge to raise electricity's share of final energy from about 23% to 35% by 2035.

Source: International Hydropower Association

Voith-led group wins equipment deal for Vietnam's first pumped storage plant

A Voith Hydro Shanghai and POWERCHINA Zhongnan consortium will supply the core electromechanical equipment for the 1,200 MW Bac Ai plant in Khanh Hoa, which has four 300 MW units. The project marks Vietnam's entry into large-scale long-duration storage.

Source: Vietnam Investment Review
