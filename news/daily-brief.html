☀️ Solar

⚡ US-made perovskite–silicon tandem modules enter commercial production

Heliene and Caelux have started commercial-scale production of a US-made module that pairs Caelux's perovskite "active glass" with conventional silicon cells. It is one of the first perovskite tandem products to move from pilot lines to real manufacturing in the US, a key test of whether the higher-efficiency chemistry can hold up at scale.

Source: PV Tech

IEA expects 3.6 TW of new solar between 2026 and 2031

The International Energy Agency's latest renewables outlook forecasts roughly 3.6 TW of new solar PV over the next six years, keeping solar as by far the largest source of new power capacity worldwide. Separately, Qcells' US-made modules passed every category in Kiwa PVEL and RETC reliability testing.

Source: PV Tech

First modules delivered to 600 MW Inner Mongolia solar project

HY SOLAR has delivered the first modules for the 600 MW Guangzhiyuan PV project in Ordos, covering about 722.6 MWp at around RMB 0.70/W. The price shows how low module costs remain in China's utility-scale buildout.

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ Sodium-ion nears cost parity with LFP, with 70 GWh under supply deals

Wood Mackenzie analysis reported by pv magazine says sodium-ion cells are closing in on the cost of lithium iron phosphate, with more than 70 GWh now covered by supply agreements. Leading commercial cells reach about 175 Wh/kg against about 205 Wh/kg for advanced LFP, according to Polytechnic University of Turin's Silvia Bodoardo. That gap is now small enough for stationary storage.

Source: ESS News

CATL bets on battery swapping to speed up sodium-ion EVs

CATL chairman Robin Zeng said its swap network, now in around 200 Chinese cities and targeting more than 3,000 passenger-car stations by year-end, will help sodium-ion batteries take hold, since they cover daily city driving. CATL expects 10,000–20,000 sodium-ion EVs on the road in 2026.

Source: CnEVPost

CALB rolls out first 588 Ah storage cells; 1 GW/4 GWh Ningxia project contracted

CALB produced its first 588 Ah grid-storage cells at a new 25 GWh-a-year plant in Zaozhuang, Shandong, the first phase of a planned 100 GWh. In Ningxia, a Wuhan Tianyuan-led consortium signed a RMB 3.64bn contract for the 1 GW/4 GWh Lingwu Huaxin shared storage project, due to be built by April 2027.

Source: TaiyangNews

💨 Wind

⚡ Vineyard Wind 1, the largest US offshore wind farm, is fully operational

The 62-turbine, 800 MW project off Massachusetts reached full commercial operation on 29 October and can power about 400,000 homes. It got there after years of delays, a 2024 blade failure and a 2025 federal stop-work order that the courts later lifted.

Source: WBUR

⚡ Ørsted's Revolution Wind hits full power as New England sets a wind record

Revolution Wind's 65 turbines (704 MW) off Rhode Island and Connecticut are now fully running and are expected to save New England ratepayers about $500m a year by 2028. Regional wind output hit a record 2,400 MW last week.

Source: WBUR

But the US offshore pipeline is thinning

With Sunrise Wind (924 MW, New York) due in 2027, there are no further Massachusetts offshore wind projects in the pipeline. That raises questions about what comes after this first wave.

Source: GBH News

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ NRC grants construction permit for Dow/X-energy SMRs at Texas chemical site

The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission approved a construction permit for four Xe-100 high-temperature gas reactors at Dow's Seadrift complex. It is the first advanced reactor approved for a US industrial site, supplying both heat and power. Dow is expected to consider a preliminary investment decision in 2027.

Source: GlobeNewswire via Manila Times

Thailand and US move toward an SMR partnership

A US-funded pre-feasibility study for state utility EGAT is under way as Thailand targets its first small modular reactor by 2037 and 9,000 MW of nuclear by 2050. Experts said the biggest hurdle is a shortage of trained regulators and operators.

Source: The Nation Thailand

♨️ Heat Pumps

⚡ Canada details its National Heat Pump Rebate: up to $10,000 per home

The federal programme opens in early 2027 with no energy audit required. It offers $2,000 per household, or up to $10,000 for low- and median-income homes. Installations from 1 November 2026 may qualify retroactively, and the scheme runs until March 2034.

Source: Mechanical Business

💧 Hydro

Voith-led consortium to equip Vietnam's first pumped storage plant

Voith Hydro Shanghai and POWERCHINA Zhongnan signed with EVN to supply the core equipment for the 1,200 MW Bac Ai plant in Khanh Hoa province (4 × 300 MW). It is a milestone for long-duration storage in Southeast Asia.

Source: International Water Power

Odisha approves 600 MW Upper Indravati pumped storage project

The state cabinet signed off on Rs 4,709 crore for the project in Kalahandi district, with MEIL as EPC contractor and commissioning targeted for FY2030-31. It is expected to supply about 1,040 GWh of peak power a year.

Source: SolarQuarter

IHA presses UK to make cap-and-floor work for pumped storage

In a letter to the government and Ofgem, IHA president Malcolm Turnbull urged that the first window of the long-duration storage regime fit how pumped hydro is financed. That window shortlisted 7.6 GW of projects, 3.9 GW of them pumped storage, including SSE's Coire Glas.

Source: International Water Power
