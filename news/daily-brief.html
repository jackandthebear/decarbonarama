Bottom line

Storage is the story this week. Sodium-ion took two serious steps toward the mainstream — Saft's joint development deal in Europe and Peak Energy's Sacramento plant in the US — while pumped hydro quietly crossed 200 GW globally, a reminder that the biggest battery on the grid is still made of water and concrete. Fusion had a genuinely large moment with Commonwealth's $1bn raise, though it remains a decade-scale bet against near-term deployment. Closer to home, the UK's £9,000 grant for oil and LPG households is the most consequential heat pump policy change of the year, aimed squarely at the homes where the economics already work. The recurring theme across wind, offshore and ocean energy is the same: the technology is largely proven, and the constraints are now ports, permits, vessels and financing.
