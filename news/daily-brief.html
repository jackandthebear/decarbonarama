Bottom line

Two milestones anchor today's brief: three terawatts of solar installed worldwide, and 200 gigawatts of pumped storage — generation and storage both crossing thresholds that would have looked implausible a decade ago. Sodium-ion is the story to watch in batteries, with CATL calling it mainstream-ready for grid duty and Saft now backing a Western supply chain; if the 15,000-cycle claims hold, lithium's grid-storage monopoly is over. Offshore wind offers the counterpoint — Dominion's flagship Virginia project slipping to end-2027 shows execution, not ambition, is the constraint. For UK homeowners the practical news is the £9,000 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant for oil and LPG properties, alongside £2bn of low-interest loans that address the upfront-cost barrier the grant alone never solved. Fusion, meanwhile, keeps raising money at a pace that suggests investors are pricing in the 2030s rather than the 2040s.
