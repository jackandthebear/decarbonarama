New Orleans council to vote on opening a plug-in "balcony solar" proceeding

New Orleans City Council's climate committee meets Monday 16 November to vote on a resolution opening a regulatory proceeding on plug-in solar, the small panels renters and apartment dwellers can plug straight into a socket. The council notes at least 30 US states have approved or are considering such policies, with 10 already authorising them, a sign the balcony-solar movement is spreading fast in the US.

Source: New Orleans City Council

The same roundup notes Qinghai is targeting 8 GW of concentrated solar power by 2030, and SOJO plans a 1.3 GW/2.6 GWh storage project in Jiangmen, a reminder of how fast China keeps pairing solar with storage.

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ Sodium-ion approaches cost parity with LFP, with 70 GWh under supply agreements

pv magazine reports that sodium-ion batteries are closing in on cost parity with lithium iron phosphate, with around 70 GWh of capacity already covered by supply deals. If parity holds, sodium-ion offers grid storage a chemistry free of lithium and its price swings.

Source: pv magazine

Grepow launches sodium-ion pouch cells for cold-climate industrial uses

Grepow released five sodium-ion pouch cells (2–7 Ah, 3.0 V nominal) on 12 November, aimed at cold-weather industrial applications. In the company's own tests one cell kept 94% capacity after 2,000 cycles versus 83% for LFP. These figures haven't been independently verified, but they show sodium-ion carving out niches where lithium struggles.

Source: ESS News

CATL ties its sodium battery push to more battery-swap stations

CATL says expanding its network of EV battery-swapping stations will help bring its sodium-ion batteries to market. The world's biggest battery maker is betting that swapping lets it roll out new chemistries without waiting for drivers to buy new cars.

Source: Bloomberg

💨 Wind

⚡ EU approves €2.04bn Belgian aid for first Princess Elisabeth Zone wind farm

The European Commission has cleared a €2.04 billion Belgian state-aid scheme for an offshore wind farm of up to 735 MW in the Princess Elisabeth Zone, expected to generate about 2.6 TWh a year. Support comes through a 20-year two-way contract for difference, with tender results expected in Q4 2027.

Source: SolarQuarter

Ørsted readies turbine installation at 2.9 GW Hornsea 3

Turbine components have reached Able Seaton Port and DEME's jack-up vessel has arrived for Hornsea 3, which will have 197 Siemens Gamesa 14 MW turbines about 120 km off Norfolk. Installation and commissioning run until November 2027 for what will be one of the world's largest offshore wind farms.

Source: offshoreWIND.biz

Connecticut's $211m offshore wind port left stranded by federal reversal

Connecticut spent more than $211 million upgrading New London's State Pier to serve offshore wind, but federal opposition has scuttled most of the planned pipeline. Completed US projects will provide about 6 GW by the end of 2027, far short of the 30 GW-by-2030 goal, and Ørsted says it doesn't expect to grow its US exposure.

Source: CT Mirror

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ Canada's flagship SMR will miss its 2030 date and $7.7bn budget

Ontario Power Generation says Darlington BWRX-300 Unit 1, a closely watched first-of-a-kind SMR, will miss its October 2030 commercial operation target and its previously disclosed $7.7 billion cost. OPG has begun a full cost and schedule review. The delay is a warning for the dozens of SMR projects that point to Darlington as their template.

Source: NucNet

⚡ IAEA World Fusion Outlook: record $4.5bn in new fusion funding

The IAEA's 2026 World Fusion Outlook, released alongside the World Fusion Energy Group meeting in Vienna, reports a record $4.5 billion in new fusion funding from July 2025 to July 2026. The number of fusion facilities worldwide has grown from 134 in 2022 to 174, and the agency describes the sector as entering a crucial transitional phase.

Source: ANS Nuclear Newswire

UK opens £115m of supplier tenders for STEP prototype fusion plant

UK Fusion Energy has invited bids for five supplier lots worth up to £115 million for STEP, its prototype fusion power plant, covering breeder blankets, fuel cycle, test operations, control systems and systems engineering. Tenders close on 29 January 2027, with contracts starting in April.

Source: World Nuclear News

♨️ Heat Pumps

EHPA: heat pumps could cut a quarter of EU energy emissions

The European Heat Pump Association says a full rollout powered by renewables would avoid about 600 Mt of CO2 a year, roughly a quarter of EU energy emissions. Europe has about 30 million residential heat pumps, about 3 million of them added in 2025. EHPA says at least 100 million are needed, against a Commission target of 4 million installs a year.

Source: Renewable Energy Magazine

Rinnai and Bosch add new residential heat pump products

Rinnai has launched new residential heat pump water heaters, and Bosch has unveiled residential air-to-air heat pumps. Both moves show major heating brands widening their heat pump lineups as demand grows.

Source: pv magazine · pv magazine India

The IHA and the Global Alliance for Pumped Storage launched a white paper at COP31 in Antalya, drawing on input from more than 60 governments. It makes seven policy recommendations to unlock "water batteries", pointing to civil works that last 80–100 years as a long-term answer to grid flexibility.
