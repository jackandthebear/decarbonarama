☀️ Solar

⚡ Perovskite finally scales: 22% efficiency on a full-size panel

A Nanjing University team with solar firm Renshine published certified full-area efficiency of 22.0% for metre-scale perovskite modules — 0.72 m² panels delivering 158.4 W under standard test conditions. Scaling perovskite from lab chips to commercial panels has always shredded performance, so a certified 22% at module size is the number the industry has been waiting on.

Source: Interesting Engineering

Sol Systems buys 200 MW Texas solar project from DESRI

Sol Systems acquired the 200 MWac Lumberton Solar Project in Hardin County, Texas, which already holds a long-term power purchase agreement. The deal moves a de-risked, contracted asset toward construction — a reminder that in ERCOT the constraint is increasingly capital and interconnection, not offtake.

Source: pv magazine USA

US on track for record 43.4 GW of utility-scale solar in 2026

The EIA expects developers to add 43.4 GW of large-scale solar PV this year, a roughly 60% jump on 2025, out of about 86 GW of total new US generating capacity. Solar alone would account for a bit over half of everything added to the grid in 2026.

Source: PV Tech

🔋 Battery Technology

⚡ China's big four battery makers converge on 2027 for solid-state trials

CALB, Gotion and others have now lined up behind BYD, CATL and Geely in targeting 2027 for small-scale solid-state cell trial production. With the top four holding over 75% of China's market, an industry-wide date turns solid-state from a lab promise into a supply-chain planning assumption.

Source: CarNewsChina

⚡ CATL signs 60 GWh sodium-ion storage deal with HyperStrong

The three-year supply agreement with Beijing HyperStrong is the largest sodium-ion storage contract announced to date. Sodium sidesteps lithium price exposure entirely and tolerates cold better — for stationary storage, where energy density matters least, it is quietly becoming the default challenger.

Source: CleanTechnica

India's first utility-scale non-lithium storage: 100 MWh flow battery at Khavda

Delectrik Systems won a tender to deploy a 100 MWh vanadium redox flow battery at the Khavda Solar Park. Flow chemistry is aimed squarely at the duration lithium handles badly — beyond the four-to-six-hour window — and this is India's first serious commercial test of it.

Source: Yale E360

💨 Wind

EnBW's He Dreiht offshore wind farm has all 64 Vestas 15 MW turbines in place, with commissioning work now underway and full operation expected by late summer. At 960 MW it is one of the largest projects built without subsidy support.

The BUS grant rose to £9,000 in July for properties off the gas grid currently running on oil or LPG, up from the standard £7,500. Combined with the EPC requirement being dropped from applications and approval times halving since 2025, the friction that stalled uptake is coming out of the scheme.

The machine, running off Aguçadoura in Portugal since 2023, picked up the first DNV Prototype Certificate ever issued to a wave energy converter. Inyanga Marine Energy Group has named the winners of contracts to build its HydroWing array off Anglesey in Wales. Alongside CfD-backed schemes from Orbital, MeyGen and Nova Innovation, it forms the most credible near-commercial tidal pipeline the sector has ever had.

Bottom line

Two long-standing "always five years away" technologies moved today: perovskite hit a certified 22% on a full-size panel, and the NRC cleared the way to restart a permanently closed US reactor for the first time. Storage is fragmenting productively — sodium-ion taking the stationary market on cost, flow batteries taking long duration, and solid-state now pinned to a shared 2027 trial date across China's biggest cell makers. Offshore wind is in its delivery phase rather than its announcement phase, with He Dreiht complete and Revolution Wind at 95%. For UK homeowners the practical news is the BUS grant at £9,000 off-grid plus £2bn of low-interest retrofit loans, which finally attacks the upfront-cost problem rather than the running-cost one. Marine energy remains small but got its first certified wave device, which is how a sector starts becoming financeable.
