Bottom line

Today's theme is unglamorous maturation. The headline numbers — 43.4 GW of US solar, 201 GW of pumped storage, 24 GW of new US batteries — are records set by boringly conventional technology deploying at scale, while the exciting stuff (fusion, solid-state, perovskite) keeps raising money and setting lab records without yet shipping. Sodium-ion is the one to watch: at 200 Wh/kg with a 60 GWh supply deal signed, it is crossing from novelty to commodity faster than most forecasts allowed. In the UK specifically, AR8 closing and the £9,000 off-gas-grid heat pump grant are the two policy levers actually moving atoms this month. And CorPower's DNV certificate is a reminder that in marine energy, paperwork milestones often matter more than power output ones.
