☀️ Solar

⚡ Avantus signs 20-year PG&E deals for 800 MW of solar and 3,200 MWh of storage

Avantus has signed two 20-year power purchase agreements with PG&E for Buttonbush 1A and 1B, the first phases of a solar-plus-storage centre in western Kern County, California. The roughly $3 billion projects need California PUC approval, would start construction in 2027 and come online in 2029, and are sited on water-stressed farmland. It's a big signal that long-duration solar-plus-storage contracting is alive and well in California.

Source: SMM News

Solarworld plans 2.4 GW solar cell plant as revenue jumps 81%

India's Solarworld Energy Solutions reported Q2 FY27 income up 81.1% year on year, though margins and profit fell. Its board approved a 50:50 joint venture with Rays Power Infra for a 2.4 GW solar cell factory in Narmadapuram, Madhya Pradesh, targeting production by June 2027. It's another step in India's push to make cells at home rather than import them.

Source: SolarQuarter

🔋 Battery Technology

IndiGrid's 250 MW / 500 MWh standalone battery goes live in Rajasthan

AmpereHour Energy integrated IndiGrid's newly commissioned two-hour battery in western Rajasthan, connected to the 765 kV Bhadla-III substation. Built from modular 40 MWh blocks, it's IndiGrid's third storage project and is designed to shift solar output into the evening peak, which India's grid badly needs.

Source: SolarQuarter

Moment Energy unveils Nova, a grid battery built from second-life EV packs

Moment Energy launched Nova, a 200 kW / 500 kWh storage block made from repurposed EV batteries, which the company says is the first second-life system certified to both UL 1973 and UL 9540. First deliveries are planned for Q1 2027. If the claims hold, it's a meaningful step for giving old EV batteries a bankable second life.

Source: GlobeNewswire

Sodium-ion edges toward cost parity with LFP as supply deals pass 70 GWh

Industry coverage this week says sodium-ion batteries are closing the gap with lithium iron phosphate, with more than 70 GWh now covered by supply agreements and utility-scale storage the main opportunity. CATL has also published updated specs and availability for its Tener Sodium storage product. A credible lithium-free chemistry would ease mineral supply risks for grid storage.

Source: pv magazine

⚛️ Nuclear

⚡ Ontario's Darlington SMR will run over budget and behind schedule

Ontario Power Generation says its first BWRX-300 small modular reactor will exceed its C$5.4 billion budget and miss its October 2030 operation target, partly because GE Vernova Hitachi is still finishing first-of-a-kind engineering. The regulator has ordered fresh settlement talks. As the West's lead SMR project, this sets expectations for followers like TVA's Clinch River.

Source: ENR

⚡ NRC authorises four X-energy reactors at Dow's Texas chemical site

The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission has approved construction of four Xe-100 high-temperature reactors, about 320 MW of electricity plus process heat, at a Dow facility in Texas, with service expected in the early 2030s. Critics at the Union of Concerned Scientists say some safety questions were deferred. It's a landmark for advanced reactors supplying industrial heat.

Source: TechRadar

IAEA: fusion is in a "crucial transitional phase"

The IAEA's World Fusion Outlook 2026 says private fusion firms raised a record $4.5 billion in the year to July 2026, but the next hurdle is integrating science, engineering, regulation and international cooperation rather than raising more capital.

Source: NucNet

♨️ Heat Pumps

New England's heat pump accelerator passes 25,000 homes in its first year

The five-state New England Heat Pump Accelerator, backed by $450 million in federal grants, now counts more than 25,000 participating homes, with nearly 90% of regional distributors signed up for midstream incentives. Its goal is 580,000 heat pumps in four years, a model for how regional programmes can move the market fast.

Source: Canary Media

Mitsubishi Electric unveils compact "The Little One" heat pump

Mitsubishi Electric has launched a new compact heat pump called "The Little One," aimed at smaller homes where space for outdoor and indoor units is tight. Smaller, easier-to-fit units remove one of the practical barriers to switching from gas boilers.

Source: pv magazine

💧 Hydro

⚡ COP31 white paper pushes pumped storage as the "homegrown" battery

The International Hydropower Association and the Global Alliance for Pumped Storage launched a white paper at COP31, shaped by more than 60 governments, with seven policy recommendations to speed up pumped hydro. It argues water batteries rely on local topography rather than imported critical minerals, tying them to the COP31 pledge to raise electricity's share of final energy to 35% by 2035.

Source: International Hydropower Association

Ireland's Silvermines pumped storage project opens final consultation

Siga-Hydro has opened the final pre-application consultation (to 21 December) for its Silvermines scheme in County Tipperary, which would reuse an old mine pit as the lower reservoir. A planning application follows, moving Ireland closer to new long-duration storage.

Source: International Water Power

🌊 Wave & Tidal

Hawaiʻi deploys "SURF-WEC" flap wave energy converter off Oʻahu

The University of Hawaiʻi and the National Laboratory of the Rockies have deployed a small DOE-funded flap-type wave energy converter off the Makai Research Pier. Wave motion drives a hydraulic generator, and the device can stay in the water for up to a year with a live public data feed, giving open real-sea performance data to the sector.

Source: University of Hawaiʻi

Eco Wave Power explores supplying wave energy to AI data centres

Eco Wave Power's US arm signed a three-year MoU with WISeQey Corp and its subsidiaries SEALSQ and SEALCOIN to explore linking renewable producers, including its Jaffa Port wave plant, to data centres and utilities. Talks with data centre developers have started, though no supply contract has been signed.
