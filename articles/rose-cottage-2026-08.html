Analysis

A month of two halves. The first fortnight was excellent — 1,026 kWh combined (68 kWh/day) with a run of 70+ kWh days peaking at 79.1 kWh on 2 August. From mid-month, conditions turned noticeably duller: the second half produced 758 kWh, with the weakest spells around 16–18, 27 and 29 August (25.9 kWh on the 29th), consistent with unsettled late-summer weather and shortening days.

Versus July. Enphase generation fell 24.9% (1,609.6 → 1,208.4 kWh) — the usual seasonal step-down from peak summer. Export dropped 28% (1,311.5 → 938.0 kWh on the Enphase meter) while imports rose ~15% (527.5 → 605.6 kWh) and home consumption crept up 6% (825.5 → 876.8 kWh), so the net-export margin narrowed but remained comfortably positive.

Array balance. The Sonnen-connected array produced 47.7% of Enphase output, right in the middle of its expected 45–50% band — both arrays are performing to spec. Solar offset for the month was 137% of household consumption (Enphase), with a CO₂ reduction equivalent of 876.5 kg.

Watch item. The Octopus meter feed in Homey went stale at 26 August ~13:00 BST (import frozen at 85,973.2 kWh, export at 9,496.9 kWh). Worth checking the Octopus integration in Homey so September's report can use metered figures for the full month.
