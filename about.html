Honest footnote: I got things wrong too. My 2022 inverter is only 3.6 kW — too small for whole-house loads. If I were doing it again I'd go bigger on both inverter and battery. That kind of lesson is exactly what this site exists to pass on.

Why the numbers here are different

Most UK energy content is written by people who have never bought the kit. Installers' blogs want to sell you a survey. The big consumer sites write from press releases. I was quoted £27,000 before the grant for a conventional air-to-water heat pump system; I heated my home a different way for around £10,000 all-in — and I publish the running data to show how it's working out.

On a good June day my panels generate over 64 kWh. The north-facing half of my roof produces about 70% of what the south side does — a number almost nobody publishes, because almost nobody metres it panel by panel. That's the standard this site aims for: numbers you can check, from a house that exists.
